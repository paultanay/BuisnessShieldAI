# Code Of Conduct

BusinessShield follows a simple standard: be professional, respectful, and useful.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat others with respect.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Give technical feedback with evidence.
- Be especially careful around legal, privacy, and security topics.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information.
- Knowingly submitting misleading compliance claims.
- Encouraging unsafe security testing.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or community.
